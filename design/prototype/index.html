Why it’s different

Eight design pillars, drawn from how ADHD actually works.

Not “TickTick with a new colour scheme.” Every feature traces back to one of these.

P1

Frictionless capture

Working memory is leaky — catch the thought in seconds, from the lock screen, your voice, or a Windows hotkey, or it’s gone.

P2

Anti-overwhelm

A long list triggers freeze. Today stays small and finite; the backlog exists but is deliberately out of sight.

P3

Initiation help

The signature layer no rival has: “Just start — 2 min,” auto-breakdown to a tiny first step, and “pick one for me.”

P4

Make time visible

Fights time-blindness — shrinking rings, time estimates, and “time left today” so not-now becomes now before it surprises you.

P5

Reminders that break through

Time-sensitive, escalating, location-aware — and they nudge again if ignored, because one silent ping never lands.

P6

Reward the brain

Dopamine by design: satisfying completion, a visible “done today,” and momentum you can feel.

P7

Be kind — no shame

Undone rolls forward gently. No red overdue pile, no guilt copy, no punishing streak resets. Kindness is retention.

P8

Goals reach down to today

Goal → project → the single next physical action, surfaced in daily planning so big goals never rot in a folder.
